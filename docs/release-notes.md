Gladsheim, a Commodore 64 RPG: an Ultima II-style overworld and towns with Bard's Tale-style first-person dungeons.

- Six dungeons on eleven floors, a puzzle in each, and Nidhogg waiting behind five runestones
- Two towns, 17 people to talk to, six side quests
- Party creation, levels 1-20, saving to disk on the map or in town
- Original SID music for the wilds and the dungeons (M turns it off; saves remember)

**Downloads**
- `gladsheim.d64` / `gladsheim.prg`: with music
- `gladsheim-nomusic.d64` / `gladsheim-nomusic.prg`: without music

In VICE: `x64sc -autostart gladsheim.d64`. On real hardware: `LOAD"GLADSHEIM",8` then `RUN`.
